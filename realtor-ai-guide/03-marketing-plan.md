# Marketing Plan: Launch "The 15-Minute Agent"


> **Read this first.** No plan guarantees sales. Treat the numbers below as *your own targets to test*, not predictions. The goal of the first 60 days is to learn what resonates, collect proof, and build an audience you own (an email list), then repeat what works.

---

## The Strategy in One Page

**Who you're selling to:** working residential agents (1–10 years in) who are curious about AI but short on time.

**Your edge:** you are a real real-estate team. Agents trust peers who do the work. Lead with *"built by a working team, for working agents"* and show the workflows in real use. (Only say this if it is true for you.)

**The funnel:**

1. **Attention:** short, useful posts and videos that show one workflow working.
2. **Free hook:** a free mini-guide (3 prompts + the SOUL-file idea) in exchange for an email.
3. **Email nurture:** 5 emails over 10 days that teach, then offer the full guide.
4. **Offer:** the guide, a launch price, and a clear guarantee.
5. **Referrals:** ask every buyer for a review and a share; pay affiliates a commission.

**Why a free mini-guide:** cold buyers rarely buy a $20–$30 digital product from a stranger the first time they see it. An email list lets you reach them again for free.

## Before You Start: Setup (Days 1–7)

### Day 1: Decide your offer and price
- [ ] Main product: *The 15-Minute Agent* PDF. Suggested launch price **$19–$29** (comparable agent-AI guides we found were about $14–$20).
- [ ] Optional upsell later: a team/office license (e.g., 5 seats) at a higher price.
- [ ] Choose a guarantee (e.g., 7-day refund). Decide it now and put it on the sales page.

### Day 2: Set up payment and delivery
- [ ] Create a **Payhip** account (free plan to start; see `04-payhip-setup-kit.md`). Connect payouts.
- [ ] Upload the PDF, add the sales copy and the delivery email.
- [ ] Do a test purchase to confirm the file arrives.

### Day 3: Build the email list tool
- [ ] Use a free email tool (e.g., MailerLite, Beehiiv, or Kit) so your list is yours if you change stores. Check each tool's free-tier limits first.
- [ ] Create a signup form for the free mini-guide.
- [ ] Add a visible unsubscribe link and your business mailing address (required by CAN-SPAM).

### Day 4: Create the free mini-guide (lead magnet)
- [ ] 3–4 pages: *"3 AI Prompts Every Agent Can Use Tomorrow"* (Morning Briefing, Listing Launch, Speed-to-Lead) plus the SOUL-file idea.
- [ ] Done: `output/FREE_3-AI-Prompts-for-Agents_Team-Smith-Homes.pdf`. After your Payhip product exists, rebuild with your link: `GUIDE_URL="https://payhip.com/b/XXXX" python3 build/build.py`.
- [ ] *Do not* reuse text from other creators' free PDFs. Use your own prompts from the guide.

### Day 5: Build one landing page
- [ ] One page, one action: enter your email, get the mini-guide.
- [ ] Headline idea: **"Get your evenings back: 3 AI prompts for busy agents."**
- [ ] Put the same link in every bio and post.

### Day 6: Set up your social profiles
- [ ] Pick **two** platforms to start (recommended: **Instagram + LinkedIn**, plus a **Facebook** presence for agent groups). Don't spread thin.
- [ ] Bio formula: *Who you help + result + link.* Example: **"Helping Realtors save 10+ hrs a week with AI. Free prompts ↓"** (Only claim hours you can back up. Use your own tracked numbers.)
- [ ] Use the Team Smith Homes logo as the profile image.

### Day 7: Create proof
- [ ] Record yourself using 2–3 workflows on a real (non-confidential) task. Screen-record with your phone.
- [ ] Time yourself: "before vs. after" is your strongest content.
- [ ] Collect 5–10 beta readers (agents you know) to try the guide free in exchange for honest feedback and a testimonial. **Only publish testimonials people actually gave you, with permission.**

## Phase 1: Build Attention (Days 8–21)

**Daily time: 30–45 minutes.** Goal: 10–14 pieces of content and your first 100 email signups.

### Posting rhythm
| Day | Format | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Short video (30–60 s) | One workflow demo |
| Tue | Carousel (5–7 slides) | A prompt + the result |
| Wed | Short video | A mistake to avoid with AI |
| Thu | Text post / story | Fair Housing + AI guardrail |
| Fri | Short video | Before vs. after, with time saved |
| Weekend | Optional | Behind the scenes, Q&A |

### 12 content ideas (use your guide)
1. "I wrote this listing in 10 minutes. Here's the prompt."
2. "Stop writing 'nestled.' Use this instead." (Show the banned-words rule.)
3. "How I text a new lead in under 60 seconds."
4. "The 20-minute setup that makes AI sound like *you*." (SOUL file)
5. "AI mistakes that could cost you your license." (Fair Housing/verification)
6. "My morning briefing prompt." (Screen recording)
7. "How to stop re-explaining yourself to AI every time." (Claude Project)
8. "Never miss a closing deadline: AI timeline tracker." (Verify dates!)
9. "5 sphere texts in 5 minutes."
10. "Renting AI vs. owning it." (Chapter 4)
11. "What I'd do in my first 30 days with AI if I were new."
12. "Free: 3 prompts you can paste today. Link in bio."

**Every post ends with one call to action.** Either "comment WORKFLOW and I'll send it" or "free prompts, link in bio."

### Use AI to make this faster
Draft captions, scripts, and carousels with Claude (this is exactly what your guide teaches). For graphics, Canva works well with your logo; the video tools connected to this workspace can help with short clips. Always review before posting.

### Where to be active (carefully)
- [ ] **Facebook groups for agents:** join 5–10. Spend 2 weeks *helping* (answer questions, share tips) before mentioning your guide. Read each group's rules; many ban promotion or allow it only on certain days.
- [ ] **LinkedIn:** post 3x/week and connect with agents, brokers, and team leaders in your market and nearby.
- [ ] **Reddit (e.g., real estate communities):** read each community's self-promotion rules first. Share value, not links.
- [ ] **Local association/brokerage events:** offer to give a 15-minute "AI for agents" talk. Be a guest, not a pitchman.

## Phase 2: Warm Up and Prove It (Days 22–35)

**Goal:** 3–5 testimonials, 200+ email subscribers (illustrative target), and a tested sales page.

- [ ] Send the beta readers a short feedback form. Ask: *What saved you the most time? What was confusing?*
- [ ] Improve the guide from what you learn. Fix confusing sections.
- [ ] Ask permission to quote feedback. Use their name/initials only if they agree.
- [ ] Post **2 case-study posts**: "Agent X cut her listing-copy time from 90 to 15 minutes" (only with real, permitted results).
- [ ] Start the **5-email nurture sequence** (below) to everyone on your list.
- [ ] Reach out to **10 potential partners** (see Partnerships).

### 5-email nurture sequence (write with the prompt coach)
| # | Timing | Goal | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Immediately | Deliver | Mini-guide + "reply with your biggest time sink" |
| 2 | Day 2 | Teach | Listing Launch walkthrough (full prompt) |
| 3 | Day 4 | Story | The real reason AI output feels generic, and the fix (SOUL file) |
| 4 | Day 7 | Proof | A testimonial or your own before/after |
| 5 | Day 10 | Offer | Introduce the full guide, price, guarantee, deadline |

*Rules for email:* real "from" name, honest subject lines, unsubscribe link, and only email people who opted in.

## Phase 3: Launch Week (Days 36–42)

**Goal:** concentrate attention for one week. Launch with a **limited-time launch price** (for example, 7 days). Only use a deadline you will actually keep.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 36 | **Teaser:** "Something's coming Friday." Post + email the list. |
| 37 | **Problem day:** content about the admin drain. Mention the guide exists. |
| 38 | **Demo day:** live or recorded demo of 2 workflows. |
| 39 | **Proof day:** testimonials and before/after results. |
| 40 | **LAUNCH:** email the list, post everywhere, update bios. Offer the launch price. |
| 41 | **FAQ day:** answer objections: "Is this Fair Housing-safe?" "Do I need a paid AI plan?" "What if I'm not techy?" |
| 42 | **Last-call:** email + post: price goes up tonight. |

**Launch checklist**
- [ ] Sales page loads on phone, checkout works, delivery email arrives.
- [ ] Refund policy visible.
- [ ] Support email monitored daily.
- [ ] Reply to every comment and DM within 24 hours during launch week.

## Phase 4: Expand Reach (Days 43–60)

**Goal:** reach new audiences without needing to post every day.

### 1) Affiliate / referral program
- [ ] Enable affiliates in Payhip (check which plan includes it and the current fees), or upgrade to Stan Store's higher tier if you prefer its affiliate tools.
- [ ] Offer **30–40%** commission (a common range; your call).
- [ ] Recruit agent coaches, real estate YouTubers, newsletter writers, and team leaders. Give them a swipe file: 3 posts, 1 email, and the logo.
- [ ] **Disclosure:** affiliates must clearly disclose their relationship (FTC rules). Put that in your affiliate instructions.

### 2) Partnerships
- [ ] Offer free copies to **10 relevant podcasters / newsletter owners / brokerage trainers** for honest reviews.
- [ ] Pitch guest spots: "How agents can use AI without hurting Fair Housing compliance."
- [ ] Offer **brokerage/team licenses** (e.g., a 5- or 10-seat price).

### 3) Paid ads (optional, small test)
- [ ] Only after you have testimonials and a working funnel.
- [ ] Start with **$5–$10/day** for 7 days to *learn*, not to profit. Target agents (Facebook/Instagram interests: real estate agent, Realtor, brokerage names).
- [ ] Send traffic to the **free mini-guide** page first, not straight to the sale.
- [ ] Stop any ad that has spent about 2x your product price with no signups or sales.
- [ ] Follow each platform's ad rules and avoid income claims.

### 4) Content that keeps working
- [ ] Turn your best posts into a **YouTube Short / Reel / TikTok** version.
- [ ] Turn each guide chapter into 3 posts. That's 20+ posts from one product.
- [ ] Add the guide link to your email signature and your Team Smith Homes website.

### 5) Reviews and referrals
- [ ] Email every buyer 7 days after purchase: "What was your biggest win? May I quote you?"
- [ ] Add a "share this with an agent friend" line to the delivery email.

## Compliance Checklist (don't skip)
- [ ] **No income or result guarantees.** Only share results you can document.
- [ ] **Testimonials:** real, permitted, and not edited to mislead.
- [ ] **Affiliate/partner disclosures** (FTC).
- [ ] **Email:** opt-in only, unsubscribe link, physical address (CAN-SPAM).
- [ ] **Third-party content:** get permission before reusing or reselling anyone else's prompts or materials (Lighthaus, Soulprint, Residual Agent).
- [ ] **Brokerage rules:** confirm your own brokerage allows outside business promotion and use of its branding. Use only the Team Smith Homes brand unless approved otherwise.
- [ ] **Fair Housing** in all examples, ads, and screenshots.
- [ ] **Privacy:** never show real client data in demos.
- [ ] Not legal advice. If unsure, ask your broker or an attorney.
