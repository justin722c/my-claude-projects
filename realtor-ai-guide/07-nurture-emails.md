## Constant Contact setup (about 20 minutes)

Plan features and screens change. Confirm on Constant Contact's pricing and help pages that your plan includes **automated email series** (sometimes called a welcome or automation flow). If it doesn't, schedule the five emails by hand using the timing below.

1. **Create a list:** "Free Guide Subscribers."
2. **Create a signup form** (name + email) with a clear promise: *"Get 3 AI prompts agents can use tomorrow."* Add a consent line: *"By signing up you agree to receive emails from Team Smith Homes. Unsubscribe any time."*
3. **Deliver the PDF:** link it on the form's thank-you page and in Email 1. Host the PDF where you can paste a public download link (Payhip free product, or upload it in Constant Contact's file library if available).
4. **Build the series:** a 5-email automation triggered when someone joins the list. Timing: Email 1 immediately, Email 2 on day 2, Email 3 on day 4, Email 4 on day 7, Email 5 on day 10.
5. **Sender details:** use a real "from" name (`[Your first name] at Team Smith Homes`) and a monitored reply-to address. Constant Contact requires your mailing address and adds the unsubscribe link; leave both in.
6. **Stop buyers from getting the pitch:** after someone buys, move or tag them so they don't keep receiving Email 5. If Constant Contact can't do that automatically on your plan, remove buyers by hand from the list each week.
7. **Send a test** to yourself and a friend. Check on your phone and in spam.

**Rules for email:** only email people who signed up. Never import a purchased list or add agents who haven't opted in. Honor unsubscribes immediately.

## Email 2: Teach (day 2)

**Subject:** The listing description you can stop rewriting
**Preview text:** One prompt, ten minutes, five pieces of content.

Hi [First Name],

Writing a listing description, then the social captions, then the email to your sphere, then the text to buyer's agents is easily an hour or more.

Here's the prompt that does all four in one pass. Paste it into Claude (or any AI tool) and fill in the brackets:

```
New listing: [ADDRESS, beds/baths/sqft, price, 3-5 real features,
one personal detail about the home or owners].

Create:
1. A 150-word MLS description. Facts only, no filler.
2. Three Instagram captions: lifestyle, data-driven, story.
3. An email to my sphere asking for referrals.
4. A 2-sentence text for buyer's agents.
5. A checklist of facts you assumed or couldn't verify.

Match my voice. Fair Housing safe: describe the property, not the people.
```

Two rules that matter:
- **Check item 5.** AI sometimes guesses. Verify every fact against your MLS data before anything goes live.
- **Describe the home, not the buyer.** "Perfect for young families" is the kind of phrase that causes Fair Housing trouble.

Try it on your next listing and reply to tell me how it went.

[YOUR FIRST NAME]
Team Smith Homes
