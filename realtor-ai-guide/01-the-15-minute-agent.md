# The 15-Minute Agent
## AI Prompts & Workflows for Realtors

*Copy. Paste. Get your afternoon back.*

---

> **Disclaimer:** This guide is education, not legal, tax, or compliance advice. AI makes mistakes. You are the licensed professional; you review everything before it goes out. Follow your brokerage's policies and your state's rules.

---

## Chapter 1 — The Problem Nobody Puts on the Business Card

You got into real estate to work with people. Then the day happens:

- the listing description you've rewritten four times,
- the lead from last night you haven't answered yet,
- the "just checking in" text you keep postponing,
- the closing timeline living half in your head and half in your inbox,
- the social post you know you should make.

None of that is selling. All of it is necessary.

**What the numbers say (read them carefully):**

- NAR's 2025 Technology Survey (1,241 responses): about **20% of agents use AI daily, 22% weekly**, and **32% haven't used it at all**. The most common use is generated content like listing descriptions (**46%**).
- Yet **46% said AI had no noticeable impact** on their business; 50% said moderately or significantly positive.
- Time-use figures floating around online (e.g., "10–15 hours a week on admin") mostly come from vendor blogs and small samples. Treat them as directional, not gospel. Track your own week; you'll learn more from that than from any statistic.

**The takeaway:** the agents getting value aren't using a secret tool. They're giving AI *context* and using *repeatable prompts*. That's all this guide is.

## Chapter 2 — 20-Minute Setup: Build Your "Agent Brain"

Single prompts are okay. A **Claude Project** that already knows you is far better — you stop re-explaining yourself every time.

**Step 1.** In Claude, create a new Project named *Agent Brain*.

**Step 2.** Paste this into the Project's instructions, filled in:

```
You are my real estate assistant. I'm a licensed agent.

ABOUT ME
Name: [NAME] | Brokerage: [BROKERAGE] | Market: [CITIES/NEIGHBORHOODS]
Specialty: [first-time buyers / luxury / investors / relocation / etc.]
Typical client: [DESCRIBE]

MY VOICE
[Paste 3 texts or emails you've actually written.]
Rules: short sentences, warm but direct, no hype.
Never use: nestled, boasts, charming, stunning, must-see, dream home.

HOW TO WORK
- Ask me for missing facts. Never invent property details, prices, or statistics.
- Draft only; I review before anything is sent.
- Never reference race, religion, familial status, disability, national origin,
  sex, or other protected characteristics when describing people or neighborhoods.
- If something needs legal or compliance review, say so.
```

**Step 3.** Add a short file or note: your active clients (first names + stage only), your active listings, and your typical transaction timeline.

> **Privacy rule of thumb:** use first names and stages, not SSNs, financial account details, or full contract PDFs unless your brokerage has approved the tool for that data.

## Chapter 3 — Seven Workflows

### Workflow 1: The Morning Briefing (5 minutes)

**When:** Before you open email.

```
Good morning. Using what you know about my business and the notes below,
give me, on one page:
1. My top 3 priorities today
2. Clients I haven't contacted in 7+ days
3. One sphere outreach to send today: who + a draft text
4. A 3-sentence market talking point for conversations or social

Notes: [Paste today's calendar, open tasks, and who you last spoke to.]
```

**Make it better:** end each day by pasting tomorrow's calendar and unfinished tasks. Your briefing writes itself.

---

### Workflow 2: Listing Launch (10 minutes instead of 90)

```
New listing: [ADDRESS, beds/baths/sqft, price, 3–5 real features,
one personal detail about the home or owners].

Create:
1. A 150-word MLS description. Facts only, no filler.
2. Three Instagram captions: lifestyle, data-driven, story.
3. An email to my sphere asking for referrals.
4. A 2-sentence text for buyer's agents.
5. A checklist of facts you assumed or couldn't verify.

Match my voice. Fair Housing safe: describe the property, not the people.
```

**Worked example (output style):**
> *"Three bedrooms, two baths, 1,850 sq ft. New roof (2023), kitchen opens to a fenced backyard with mature pecan trees. Walk to Cole Park..."*

**Always verify:** square footage, school names, HOA terms, and anything you didn't personally confirm. Item 5 exists to catch AI guesses. **Never** let a listing go live without checking it against your MLS data.

---

### Workflow 3: Speed-to-Lead Follow-Up (3 minutes)

The first agent to respond usually has the advantage. AI helps you respond *well*, fast.

```
A new lead came in. Here is what I know: [name, source, what they asked,
price range, timeline].

Write:
1. A text under 40 words that answers their actual question
   and asks ONE easy question.
2. An email version with a clear next step (call or showing).
3. A follow-up to send if they don't reply in 24 hours.
4. A follow-up for day 4 that gives value (not "just checking in").
```

**Tip:** save your best-performing replies into the Agent Brain so it learns what works for *your* market.

---

### Workflow 4: Sphere (SOI) Check-Ins

```
I need to text [FIRST NAME]. What I know: [last time we talked,
something personal, where they are in their housing journey].

Write a text that:
- sounds like me, not a mass blast
- mentions something real about them
- is under 3 sentences
- makes no sales ask
- ends with something that's easy to reply to
Give me 3 variations.
```

**Make it better:** keep a running notes doc of personal details (kids' sports, pets, new jobs). Paste the relevant lines in. Specificity is the entire trick.

---

### Workflow 5: Transaction Deadline Tracker

```
Here are the key dates and terms from my contract (typed in, not the PDF):
[effective date, option period, financing deadline, inspection, appraisal,
closing date, other contingencies].

Create:
1. A dated timeline of every deadline
2. A weekly checklist for the next 14 days
3. A client-friendly email explaining "what happens next"
4. Questions I should confirm with my broker or TC
```

**Guardrail:** AI can mis-calculate dates and doesn't know your state's contract nuances. Verify every date against the actual contract.

---

### Workflow 6: Client Q&A Replies

```
A client asked: "[PASTE QUESTION]"
Context: [stage, relevant facts].

Write a reply that is clear, calm, and under 120 words.
Explain the concept simply. Tell me if this needs a lawyer,
lender, or broker input before I send it.
```

Great for: "What's an appraisal gap?", "Why did the inspector flag this?", "Should we waive a contingency?" (here, AI drafts the explanation; **you** give the advice.)

---

### Workflow 7: The Weekly Market Post

```
Write a 120-word market update for [AREA]. Use ONLY these numbers:
[median price, days on market, inventory, months of supply — from your MLS].
Tone: plain English, no predictions. End with one question that invites a reply.
Also give me a 20-second video script version.
```

**Rule:** AI has no live MLS access. Feed it your own data, cite the source in the post.

## Chapter 4 — Guardrails (The Part That Protects Your License)

1. **Fair Housing.** Describe properties, not people. Avoid phrases like "perfect for young families" or "safe neighborhood." Ask AI to review drafts: *"Flag any language that could violate Fair Housing."* Still check it yourself.
2. **Verify facts.** Prices, dates, square footage, taxes, school info, flood zones, and legal claims all get checked at the source.
3. **Privacy.** Don't paste sensitive client data into tools your brokerage hasn't approved.
4. **Brokerage/MLS rules.** Some require approval or disclosure for AI-generated content, and virtual staging has specific MLS disclosure rules in many areas. Ask your broker.
5. **No legal or financial advice.** AI drafts the explanation; refer the question to the right professional.
6. **Be honest.** Never fabricate testimonials, reviews, or stats.

## Chapter 5 — Your 30-Day Plan

| Week | Do this | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build Agent Brain; run Morning Briefing daily | 20 min + 5/day |
| 2 | Use Listing Launch on your next listing; add Speed-to-Lead replies | 15 min/day |
| 3 | Run 5 SOI texts per day; try the Deadline Tracker on one live deal | 15 min/day |
| 4 | Weekly Market Post; review what worked and update your Brain | 30 min |

**Measure it:** write down hours of admin this week vs. week 4. If it didn't save time, change the prompts, not your expectations.
