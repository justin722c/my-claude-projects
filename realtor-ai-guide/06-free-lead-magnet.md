# 3 AI Prompts Every Agent Can Use Tomorrow

## Before you paste: give AI something to work with

Most agents get generic AI output for one reason: the AI knows nothing about them. Fix that once and every prompt below gets better.

**Open a new Claude Project (or a saved chat) and paste in 5 lines about you:**

```
My name, brokerage, and market: [DETAILS]
Who I work with: [first-time buyers / sellers / investors / relocation]
How I talk: [paste 2-3 real texts or emails you've written]
Words I'd never use: [nestled, boasts, charming, stunning, must-see]
My rule: never invent facts. Ask me when something is missing.
```

For a deeper version, the free **Soulprint** tool from the Free Agent Movement walks you through a 10-minute interview and produces a written "SOUL file" in your own words that you can paste into any AI tool.

> **Always:** you review everything before it's sent. AI drafts; you decide.

## Prompt 1: The Morning Briefing (5 minutes)

**Use it:** before you open email.

```
Good morning. Using what you know about my business and the notes below,
give me, on one page:
1. My top 3 priorities today
2. Clients I haven't contacted in 7+ days
3. One sphere outreach to send today: who + a draft text
4. A 3-sentence market talking point for conversations or social

Notes: [paste today's calendar, open tasks, and who you last spoke to]
```

**Make it better:** at the end of each day, paste tomorrow's calendar and unfinished tasks. Your briefing nearly writes itself.

## Prompt 2: The Listing Launch (10 minutes instead of 90)

**Use it:** when a new listing is ready.

```
New listing: [ADDRESS, beds/baths/sqft, price, 3-5 real features,
one personal detail about the home or owners].

Create:
1. A 150-word MLS description. Facts only, no filler.
2. Three Instagram captions: lifestyle, data-driven, story.
3. An email to my sphere asking for referrals.
4. A 2-sentence text for buyer's agents.
5. A checklist of facts you assumed or couldn't verify.

Match my voice. Fair Housing safe: describe the property, not the people.
```

**Always verify:** square footage, school names, HOA terms, and anything you didn't confirm yourself. Check the final copy against your MLS data. Item 5 exists to catch AI guesses.

## Prompt 3: Speed-to-Lead Reply (3 minutes)

**Use it:** the moment a new lead comes in.

```
A new lead came in. Here is what I know: [name, source, what they asked,
price range, timeline].

Write:
1. A text under 40 words that answers their actual question
   and asks ONE easy question.
2. An email version with a clear next step (call or showing).
3. A follow-up to send if they don't reply in 24 hours.
4. A follow-up for day 4 that gives value (not "just checking in").
```

**Make it better:** save the replies that get answers into your Project. It learns what works in *your* market.

## Three rules that protect your license
1. **Fair Housing:** describe properties, not people. Ask AI to flag risky language, then check it yourself.
2. **Verify facts:** prices, dates, square footage, and legal claims come from the source, never from AI.
3. **Privacy:** don't paste sensitive client data into tools your brokerage hasn't approved.
